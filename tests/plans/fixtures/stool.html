Simple Step Stool

A sturdy stool for the kitchen.

Tools

Cordless drill, jig saw, tape measure

Cut List

2 - 3/4 x 11-1/4 x 9" Sides
1 - 3/4 x 11-1/4 x 16" Top
1 - 3/4 x 3-1/2 x 14-1/2" Stretcher
(2) 1x4 @ 14-1/2" Aprons
1 - 3/4 x 5-1/2 x 14-1/2" Step

Instructions

1. Cut all parts to length.
2. Cut a V notch in the bottom of each side.
3. Drill pocket holes in the aprons and stretcher.
4. Attach the aprons and stretcher to the sides.
5. Screw the top on and sand smooth.
